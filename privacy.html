Privacy Policy

HookVault · Last updated: 27 November 2026

HookVault sells a digital download pack (video hooks and caption templates for content creators). This page explains what information is involved when you visit this site or buy the product.

Payments

Payments are processed by our payment provider (Polar or Creem, depending on the checkout you use). We never see, receive, or store your card or bank details. The provider shares with us only the information needed to deliver your purchase and keep sales records: your email address, the product purchased, the amount paid, and the transaction status.

Delivery

Your files are delivered as a download after payment. We use your email address only to deliver the product, send purchase-related messages, and provide support.

Analytics

This site loads a small visit counter script from ship-or-die.com because HookVault participates in the Ship or Die hackathon, which verifies site traffic. It is used to count visits to this page. We do not run advertising trackers and we do not sell any data.

Data we hold

  • Order records provided by the payment provider (email, product, amount, status).
  • Emails you send us if you contact support.

We do not collect accounts, passwords, or payment credentials on this site.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what order data we hold about you, and ask for it to be deleted, by emailing us. We will respond within 30 days.
